Registering Oceanids Events

As a UC-affiliated support group, Oceanids events are protected by University Liability Policies.  Whether the event is a campus meeting, an interest group meeting, a Wednesday Coffee, an All Aboard trip, or any of our planned events such as the Fall Brunch, Theater Event, or Holiday Party, the event must be registered to ensure liability coverage and should be registered AT LEAST 7 DAYS BEFORE the event.

To register your event, simply,

  • Print the Registration Instructions to have on hand to help you complete the online registration process

  • To fill out the online application, visit                               https://amba-usweb.com/usweb/#/fas/eventLiabilityStep1 (This is a new link!)

  • Or print and fill out form from https://ucsd.campusconnexionsuc.com/app/uploads/amba-pdf-library/UC-FAS-Emeriti-Retiree-Application.pdf  (This is a new link!)

  • Complete the online application using the Registration Instructions above

  • Print your Certificate of Insurance that will be emailed to you upon completion of the registration of your event or print/save certicate and

  • Email or forward the Certificate of Insurance to Beate Menzel, jrblmenzel@cox.net and cc: Alyssa Brambilam abrambila@ucsd.edu

If you difficulty registering your event, contact Alyssa Brambila, UCSD Support Group Office, 858-534-8196 or abrambila@ucsd.edu, M - F:  7am -3:30pm

****  If you cannot complete your application online, you may complete the registration form and submit the completed form to:

plsdsteam.service@getamba.com 

or send to:  Campus Connexions Program Administrator: Association Member Benefits & Insurance Agency, P.O. Box 14521, Des Moines, IA 50306
