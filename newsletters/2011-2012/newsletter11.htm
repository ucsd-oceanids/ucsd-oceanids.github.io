Oceanids News

Oceanids News, our newsletter formerly known as Bear Facts is published bi-monthly during the academic year.  All submission of articles of interest for consideration of publication must be received by the 15th of the month of the next issue. 

Address all submissions to:  Oceanids News, International Center, MC 0049, 9500 Gilman Dr., La Jolla, CA  92093-0049, or email to:  oceanids@ucsd.edu or renright@ucsd.edu

 

A pdf viewer is needed to view or download newsletters.  If you do not have a viewer, you may download Adobe's Acrobat Reader    (free).
