Giving

Oceanids started at Scripps Oceanography in 1952 and its members have witnessed the birth and amazing growth of UC San Diego since it was founded in 1960.  This renowned university brings us together and Oceanids keeps us in touch with one another and the broader UC San Diego family.  Our members share a common belief in the power of higher education, and awarding scholarships and fellowships beautifully underscores this.  Over time four endowments have been created, and thanks to the annual gifts from our members, and the wise management of the UC San Diego Foundation, these endowments continue to grow.

In 2022, Oceanids members have awarded a record number of 16 Graduate Fellowships totaling $41,500.  In addition, Oceanids awarded six Undergraduate Service Awards, one for each college, to students selected by their Dean.

And that's not all.  Oceanids members established an Emergency Fund for International Students to help with sudden unexpected monetary crises. Money donated to our General Fund, has supported the Student Run Free Clinic and Oceanids is a sponsor for the UCSD Post Doctoral Association's annual STEM Career Symposium.

Each year thanks to your gifts, our Oceanids scholarship and fellowships enable students to step out into the world with a lighter financial burden. And our program support further assists them in their endeavors.
